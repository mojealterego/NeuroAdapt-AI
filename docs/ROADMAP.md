# NeuroAdapt AI — Roadmap

Source: *Projekt Grantowy Unity For Humanity*, 10-page concept document. This roadmap tracks proposals, **not completed work**.

## Phase 1 — Months 1–4: pre-production
- [ ] Co-design with stroke survivors, prosthesis users and clinical specialists
- [ ] Define intended medical use, exclusions, clinical safety envelope and risk management
- [ ] Ethics approvals, consent, clinical protocol and research governance
- [ ] Unity XR platform selection, accessibility requirements and device matrix
- [ ] Threat model, privacy impact assessment, data minimization and retention policy
- [ ] Confirm grant eligibility, applicant legal status, partners and budget

## Phase 2 — Months 5–10: alpha
- [ ] Unity XR Interaction Toolkit scene and accessible interaction framework
- [ ] EMG adapter with calibration, signal quality and disconnect handling
- [ ] Hand tracking and alternative input (gaze, controllers, accessible UI)
- [ ] Therapy modules: virtual mirror, movement exercises and gamified environments
- [ ] A.U.R.A. deterministic safe baseline, bounded difficulty controller and offline inference experiments
- [ ] Synthetic datasets with provenance and reproducible training pipeline
- [ ] Encrypted local persistence, optional consent-based synchronization and audit controls

## Phase 3 — Months 11–16: beta / proposed clinical pilot
- [ ] Performance, cybersickness and accessibility verification on actual hardware
- [ ] Clinical-grade outcome measures and validated questionnaires
- [ ] Clinician review workflow and adverse-event handling
- [ ] Ethics-approved pilot design (concept proposes 100 participants / 3 months; feasibility unverified)
- [ ] Usability studies and independent security assessment

## Phase 4 — Months 17–20: evaluation
- [ ] Analyze study outcomes with prespecified statistical methods
- [ ] Model drift, bias, subgroup and safety evaluation
- [ ] Low-power device optimization and offline resilience testing
- [ ] Review clinical effectiveness claims against actual evidence

## Phase 5 — Months 21–24: regulatory and distribution
- [ ] Determine jurisdiction-specific medical device classification and conformity route
- [ ] Prepare technical documentation, clinical evaluation and post-market processes as applicable
- [ ] Validate NGO / clinic distribution, localization and support
- [ ] Establish B2B and subsidized access governance; validate CSR impact model

## Acceptance gates
Every item needs linked evidence, tests, owner and review. No phase may be marked complete based on documentation alone.

## Proposed impact metrics (targets, not results)
- 30% movement / signal improvement for 80% of active participants
- 4-point average reduction in reported phantom pain on the specified scale
- 75% adherence to at least 3 sessions per week
- 50 partner clinics in low-income regions

These targets require protocol-level operational definitions, baseline measurement, clinical feasibility and independent verification.
