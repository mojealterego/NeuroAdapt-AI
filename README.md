# NeuroAdapt AI

Immersive neuromotor rehabilitation and prosthetic adaptation research platform.

> **Status: research and engineering development. Not a certified medical device; not for unsupervised clinical use.**

## Scope
- Unity XR rehabilitation environments and accessible interactions
- EMG and hand-tracking input adapters
- A.U.R.A. (Adaptive User Recovery Assistant), with bounded, clinician-configurable difficulty adaptation
- Offline-first operation and privacy-preserving clinical research data workflows
- Evaluation, accessibility, security, and medical-device regulatory readiness

## Repository layout
- `docs/`: architecture, roadmap, safety and research requirements
- `schemas/`: versioned interoperable data contracts
- `tests/`: automated validation

## Engineering rules
No personal health data in logs or public repositories. Simulated data only until ethics, legal and security approvals are documented. AI recommendations cannot override clinical safety limits. Clinical effectiveness, regulatory clearance and grant eligibility remain unverified.

See [ROADMAP](docs/ROADMAP.md) and [Architecture](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).
